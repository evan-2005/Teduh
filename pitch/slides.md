# TEDUH: slide outline

One idea per slide. Paste into your slide tool; screenshots come from the running app.

## 1. Title
**Teduh.** Small changes. Cooler journeys. *Teduh* is Malay for shaded or sheltered. Track: Planet, climate resilience.

## 2. The problem
A township can already have trees and footpaths and still have exposed gaps between them.
Managers have a small budget and no way to see which gap matters most.
*Visual:* the real SS 15 map around the LRT station, with the Before side in amber (paths in direct sun).

## 3. The decision we support
"With our budget, which shade improvements would help the most everyday journeys?"
Our hypothesis, tested rather than assumed: **connecting existing shaded sections can beat spreading new shade evenly.**

## 4. Demo (live, 3 minutes)
Follow `demo-script.md`. Backup video ready.

## 5. How it works
Map the paths and shade at four times of day → add 6–10 candidate trees or shelters → set budget and priorities → compare every affordable combination → recommend, explain, and hand off a build pack.
*Visual:* the control panel and results panel.

## 6. What is different
Shade-aware routing already exists (for example Singapore's Undercover). TEDUH adds:
- township-scale **budgeted planning** (not just routing)
- **timing** (09:00 to 17:00 snapshots) and **canopy growth** (young trees vs shelters)
- **maintenance** costs alongside install costs
- a **fabrication hand-off** (bill of materials, drawing, CSV/JSON)
- a **before/after** view so the result can be checked

## 7. Honesty
- Shade is not comfort or safety. We claim no temperature drop.
- Every number carries a source badge: surveyed, estimated, ai-confirmed, missing.
- AI estimates are labelled and need a human to confirm. The route and budget maths is inspectable, with no black box.
- Fabrication output is indicative and needs engineer sign-off.
- Prototype figures are illustrative, not measurements.

## 8. Pilot plan
1. Survey one real neighbourhood (paths, shade at four times, 6–10 candidate spots).
2. Compare predicted shade with observed shade after a small intervention.
3. Count pedestrians and collect resident feedback before and after.
4. Replace planning costs with contractor quotes.

## 9. Data and honesty about it
Streets, buildings and places come from OpenStreetMap; shade is modelled from the real sun position and mapped buildings. OpenStreetMap has no trees here, so midday shade is understated. The pilot fixes that with a field survey, and the AI shade scan (human-confirmed) speeds it up.

## 10. Resident voice
Use 3–5 **real** quotes from `resident-interviews.md`. Do not paraphrase into claims the resident did not make.

## 11. Ask / next steps
What you need from the judges or township partner: access to one neighbourhood, a landscape contact, and a maintenance budget line.
