# Resident interviews

**Only put real quotes here.** Do not invent, polish into new claims, or attribute to anyone who did not say it. Ask permission to quote, and use first names or "resident, age band" only.

## Questions (5 minutes each)
1. What places do you walk to most days? At what times?
2. Which part of that walk feels most exposed to the sun? Why?
3. Have you changed your route, your timing, or how you travel because of it?
4. If one small thing could change on that walk, what would it be?
5. Is there anything on the path that blocks you (parked bikes, broken paving)?
